01 / ABOUT

A multidisciplinary path
toward human disease genetics.

My research journey spans biology, genetics, parasite epidemiology, immunoinformatics, and computational genomics.

My training includes a Ph.D. in Genetics from the University of KwaZulu-Natal, an M.Sc. in Genetics from the University of Lagos, and a B.Tech. (Hons) in Biology from Ladoke Akintola University of Technology. My research experience spans parasite genetics and epidemiology, computational immunoinformatics, and human genetic epidemiology.

My current research direction centers on applying statistical and computational methods to understand complex human diseases, identify shared genetic influences, and connect genetic signals to underlying biology.

I value interdisciplinary research that brings together robust statistical analysis, biological interpretation, and collaborative science.
